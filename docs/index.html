A podcast editor for the Mac

Edit audio by editing text.

Wave transcribes your recording on your Mac. Cut words in the transcript and the audio follows. Then clean up the sound, and see who said what.

Download for Mac

Version 1.1.0, 19 MB. For Macs with Apple Silicon and macOS 13.4 or later.

Wave isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so its first launch needs you to allow it once in Privacy & Security.

Wave is free, and nothing leaves your Mac. Built by Saleh Kayyali Source

What Wave does

Editing

  • Delete words in the transcript and they’re cut from the audio, joined with a short crossfade. What you hear is what you export.
  • Whisper writes what was said, “um”s and “uh”s included (English), and every word is aligned to its exact audio.
  • Trim on the waveform to the millisecond, for a breath or the tail of a word.
  • Correct a misheard word in place. The correction carries into every export.
  • Find a phrase with ⌘ F, and cut its matches one by one.
  • Shorten long pauses to a Tight, Natural or Relaxed rhythm, or add a pause, filled with the room’s own quiet.
  • Undo anything. The original recording is never changed.

Sound

  • Clean up removes background noise and room echo (DPDFNet), at Subtle, Medium or Full strength.
  • EQ presets for the voice, each named for what it fixes: Clearer, Warmer, Less boom, Less boxy, Less nasal, Brighter, Less harsh and Broadcast.
  • Loudness for podcast delivery: Apple Podcasts, Spotify and YouTube, or broadcast.
  • Preview 15 seconds against the original first, and switch between the two while playing.

Speakers

  • Speaker detection finds who speaks when, for up to 8 people (NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Diarization).
  • Rename or merge speakers, and select or delete everything one person said.
  • Names carry into TXT, SRT and WebVTT exports.

Export

  • MP3, AAC, WAV or FLAC, plus the transcript as TXT, SRT, WebVTT or JSON, all with your cuts.

On your Mac

  • Everything runs on Apple Silicon. No account, no cloud, no upload.
  • Wave updates itself: it checks quietly, shows what’s new, and asks before installing.

Edit with an AI agent

Wave includes an MCP server, so an AI agent like Claude or ChatGPT can read your transcript and make the same edits you can: cut filler words and false starts, tighten pauses, fix misspelled names, clean up the sound and export. Each edit appears in Wave as it’s made, marked with the agent’s name, and ⌘ Z undoes it.

“In Episode 12, remove the filler words and false starts, tighten the pauses, and enhance the audio with the Clearer EQ.”

Set it up

  1. In Wave, open Settings → AI agents and turn it on. It shows the exact setup for your copy of Wave.
  2. Add Wave to your agent app once:

    Claude Code

    claude mcp add --scope user wave -- /Applications/Wave.app/Contents/MacOS/wave mcp

    Claude Desktop: Settings → Developer → Edit Config, add this, then restart Claude.

    { "mcpServers": { "wave": { "command": "/Applications/Wave.app/Contents/MacOS/wave", "args": ["mcp"] } } }

    Codex, or the ChatGPT desktop app in its Codex and Work modes

    codex mcp add wave -- /Applications/Wave.app/Contents/MacOS/wave mcp
  3. Keep Wave open, and ask.

Good to know

  • Agents work from the transcript, so their AI service sees that text. The audio stays on your Mac.
  • Agents can’t hear the audio. They judge from the words, their timing and the pauses, so give the result a listen.
  • ChatGPT in the browser can’t reach apps on your Mac, so it can’t use Wave.

Install Wave

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later)
  • macOS 13.4 (Ventura) or later
  • About 2 GB free for the speech models
  • An internet connection once, to download them

Steps

  1. Download the DMG, open it, and drag Wave into Applications.
  2. The first launch. Wave isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so the first time you open it, macOS stops it and says it can’t verify the developer. That’s expected:
    1. Click Done.
    2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to the message about Wave.
    3. Click Open Anyway, then Open.

    You only do this once, and updates don’t ask again. Or, in Terminal:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Wave.app
  3. Click Set up transcription and download Transcription (1.6 GB). Clean-up (14 MB) and speaker detection (115 MB) are optional, in Settings.
  4. Drop in a recording: MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC and more.

Updates

Wave 1.1 and later update themselves. Wave checks quietly, shows what’s new, and installs only when you choose. It never restarts without asking.

Speech models

  • Whisper Large v3 Turbo by OpenAI, through whisper.cpp (MIT).
  • wav2vec2 by Meta, for word alignment (Apache-2.0).
  • DPDFNet by Ceva, for clean-up (Apache-2.0).
  • Nemotron 3 Diarization by NVIDIA, for speakers (OpenMDW 1.1).

Audio

MP3 export uses LAME 3.100, bundled as a separate library under the LGPL. Its source is at sourceforge.net/projects/lame.
