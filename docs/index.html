How it worksGitHub ↗

Make the cut in the transcript

Wave turns your recording into words. Select what should go, hear the edit, then fine-tune it in the waveform.

Download for Mac

Free to download · Apple Silicon · macOS 13.4 or later

Less time finding the edit

Start with the words

Drop in a recording. Select a filler, a false start or a whole passage, and delete it from the audio.

Get the timing right

Switch between Flow and Segments. Zoom into the waveform to trim a breath, adjust a cut or add a pause.

Listen, then export

Preview your cuts. Save the finished audio as MP3, AAC, WAV or FLAC, with a transcript or subtitles if you need them.

Your recording stays on your Mac

Transcription and audio processing run locally. There’s no account to create and no recording to upload.

Choose Whisper for broad language support, or Parakeet for English and 24 other European languages. Download your model once, then work offline.

Make a conversation easier to follow

Find speaker turns, name the voices and clean up background noise. Every edit can be undone.

Bring your AI agent along

Connect an agent through Wave’s MCP server to find passages, tighten pauses and make edits you can review in the editor.

A little more room for the conversation

Get Wave for Mac ↓

Apple Silicon Macs, M1 or later
About 681 MB for Parakeet or 1.6 GB for Whisper

Getting started

  1. Open the download and drag Wave into Applications.
  2. If macOS blocks the first launch, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.
  3. Choose and download a transcription model in Wave’s Settings, then add your recording.
