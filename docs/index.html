A podcast editor for the Mac

Edit audio by editing text.

Wave transcribes your recording on your Mac. Cut words in the transcript and the audio follows. Then clean up the sound, and see who said what.

Download for Mac

Version 1.1.0, 19 MB. For Macs with Apple Silicon and macOS 13.4 or later.

Wave isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so its first launch needs you to allow it once in Privacy & Security.

01Editing

Cut words. The audio follows.

Wave turns your recording into a transcript you can edit like a document. Every change is a cut in the audio, joined with a short crossfade, and the original is never touched.

  • Whisper writes what was said, “um”s included (English), and places every word on its audio.
  • Trim on the waveform to the millisecond, for a breath or the tail of a word.
  • Shorten long pauses to a natural rhythm, or add one, filled with the room’s own quiet.
  • Export MP3, AAC, WAV or FLAC, and the transcript as TXT, SRT, WebVTT or JSON, all with your cuts.

02Sound

Clean up the sound.

Remove background noise and room echo, shape the voice, and set the loudness for where it’s going. Preview 15 seconds first, then switch between the original and the cleaned-up version while it plays.

  • Clean up with DPDFNet, at Subtle, Medium or Full strength.
  • EQ presets named for what they fix, from a muddy voice to sharp S sounds.
  • Loudness for Apple Podcasts, Spotify and YouTube, or broadcast.

03Speakers

Know who’s speaking.

Speaker detection works out who speaks when, for up to 8 people, on your Mac. Each turn becomes a paragraph under its speaker’s name.

  • Rename speakers, or merge two that are really one.
  • Select everything one person said, and delete it in one step.
  • Names carry into TXT, SRT and WebVTT exports.

AndAI agents

Or ask an agent.

Wave includes an MCP server, so Claude or ChatGPT can read your transcript and make the same edits you can. Each edit appears as it’s made, marked with the agent’s name, and undoes like your own.

claude mcp add --scope user wave -- /Applications/Wave.app/Contents/MacOS/wave mcp

First turn it on in Wave, under Settings → AI agents. Agents work from the transcript, so their AI service sees that text. The audio stays on your Mac.

04Install

Get Wave.

  1. Open the DMG and drag Wave into Applications.
  2. The first launch. Wave isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS stops it once and says it can’t verify the developer. That’s expected:
    1. Click Done.
    2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about Wave, and click Open Anyway, then Open.
    Or, in Terminal:xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Wave.app
  3. Click Set up transcription and download Transcription (1.6 GB). Clean-up (14 MB) and speaker detection (115 MB) are optional.
  4. Drop in a recording: MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC and more.
