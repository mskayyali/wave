Get Wave.

Free and open source. For Macs with Apple Silicon and macOS 13.4 or later.

Download for Mac

Version 1.1.0, 19 MB.

Made by Saleh Kayyali. The source is on GitHub.

  1. Open the DMG and drag Wave into Applications.
  2. The first time you open it, macOS stops it. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. In Wave’s Settings, download Transcription (1.6 GB). Then drop in a recording.
